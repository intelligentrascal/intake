# Intake 1.4.0 — Offline renaming

## New

- **Rename files from their contents, offline.** Intake can now download a free model that runs entirely on your Mac and names PDFs and images from what's in them, for example `2026-09-14 Invoice Acme September Hosting.pdf`. No account, no API key, and nothing about your files leaves your Mac. It's the new default naming provider. Apple Intelligence and OpenRouter are still available in Settings › AI.
- **Sized for your Mac.** Intake recommends one download:
  - Best — 2.5 GB download · most accurate
  - Standard — 1.28 GB download · accurate for everyday files
  - Lite (CPU) — 533 MB · renames when plugged in (Intel Macs)

  You can choose another size, change it later, or delete it from Settings › AI.
- **A setup window that stays out of the way.** It opens on first launch. You can skip it, and **Set Up Offline Renaming…** stays in the menu bar. The download shows as a ring on the menu bar icon, can be paused or resumed, asks before using a metered connection, and picks up where it left off after a quit, sleep or lost connection. If you cancel, you can resume from Settings within 24 hours.
- **Checked before it's used.** A downloaded model is installed only after its size and checksum match and a short test passes on your Mac.
- **Smart about power.** Settings › AI › Run the offline model: Smart (recommended), Always, or Only when plugged in. With Smart, Intake renames right away on power. On battery it renames single new files and saves bigger jobs for when you plug in. It also pauses when your Mac is hot, low on memory or in Low Power Mode.
- **Waiting to rename.** A file that can't be renamed yet still gets its tidy Title Case name and is filed on schedule. It's listed as *Waiting to rename* in the menu bar and Activity, and later renamed wherever it was filed ("Renamed after filing", with Undo). If you're in a hurry, click **Rename Now** or **Rename All Now**.

## Changed

- The content-aware naming provider "On this Mac" is now called **Apple Intelligence**. The provider order is Offline model, Apple Intelligence, OpenRouter.
- When you upgrade, a one-time sheet explains offline renaming (**Set Up…** / **Not Now**). Your current provider stays as it is:
  - If you use Apple Intelligence, you switch to the offline model only when its download is verified and you leave **Use the offline model for renaming** checked.
  - If you use OpenRouter, you stay on OpenRouter unless you choose otherwise.
- If you skip setup, Intake reminds you once, 7 days later. It sends a notification only if you already allow them; otherwise it shows a small dot in the menu bar.

## Privacy

- The offline model never uses the network to rename. Downloading the model is the only network request, made over HTTPS to Hugging Face, and it sends nothing about your files.
- Files waiting to rename are remembered by path only, never by their contents.
- If the offline model isn't ready, files keep their Title Case names. Intake never switches to OpenRouter on its own.

## Requirements

- macOS 26 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. The offline model needs 0.5–2.5 GB of free disk space, depending on the size you choose.
